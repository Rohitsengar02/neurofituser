Terms of Service

Terms and conditions governing the use of NeuroFit mobile app, services, and coaching platform.

1. Commitment to Privacy

Welcome to NeuroFit ("we," "our," or "us"). We prioritize the security and confidentiality of your personal information. This Terms of Service details how we handle the information collected when you use our mobile application, fitness tracking services, AI coach features, and web portal.

2. Information We Collect

To deliver personalized workout generation, nutrition guidance, and health metrics, we collect:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, password credentials, and optional social/Google profile data.
  • Fitness & Health Profiles: Age, biological sex, height, weight, target fitness goals, activity levels, and dietary preferences.
  • Sensor & Activity Data: Step count, walking speed, active calorie burn, and workout duration via on-device sensors (e.g., Pedometer & Activity Recognition).
  • Transactional & Order Records: Purchase history from the NeuroFit Marketplace (payment details processed via secure 256-bit tokenized gateways like Stripe/Razorpay).

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To synthesize dynamic, adaptive workout regimens and AI-driven coaching suggestions.
  • To track workout consistency, streaks, and calculate leaderboard reward points.
  • To prevent fraud, verify verified trainer accreditations, and protect account integrity.
  • We NEVER sell or lease your health, workout, or biometric records to third-party advertisers.

4. Data Security & Storage

All sensitive communications between the NeuroFit client and our infrastructure utilize TLS 1.3 encryption. At-rest databases employ AES-256 cryptographic standards. You may request full account deletion and data export at any time from the app Settings or by contacting our support.
