Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Last Updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

NeuroFit ("we", "our", or "us") provides mobile and web athletic fitness tracking, AI workout recommendations, nutrition analytics, and personal training platform services. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, store, protect, and handle your information when you use the NeuroFit mobile application and web services.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect data strictly necessary to deliver personalized training and nutrition algorithms:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, password hash, and profile picture.
  • Fitness & Physical Metrics: Height, weight, target goals, workout logs, sets, reps, weight lifted, and activity duration.
  • Nutrition & Dietary Data: Meal logs, calorie counts, macro ratios (protein, carbs, fat), and dietary restrictions.
  • Device & Sensor Permissions: Step counts (Pedometer sensor), accelerometer activity tracking, and optional Bluetooth heart rate monitor connectivity.
  • Transactions & Billing: Subscription status, order fulfillment history (handled via encrypted PCI-DSS compliant processors like Stripe/Razorpay).

🔒 Zero Data Selling: We do NOT sell, rent, or monetize your health, workout, or biometric data to third-party data brokers or advertisers.

3. Device Permissions & Purpose

The NeuroFit app requests the following device permissions solely for functional workout tracking:

  • ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION / SENSORS: To accurately count daily steps and calculate active calorie burn.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: To send workout reminders, coach feedback, and order delivery status.
  • INTERNET / NETWORK: To sync workout history and access live nutrition facts.

4. Data Security & Storage

All transmitted data is protected using industry-standard TLS 1.3 encryption. Biometric and workout archives are stored in encrypted databases hosted on certified cloud infrastructure with strict access controls.

5. User Rights & Account Deletion

You maintain full ownership of your data. You may at any time:

  • Export your complete workout and nutrition history in CSV/JSON format.
  • Request permanent deletion of your account and all associated records directly from the App Settings or by contacting privacy@neurofit.app.
